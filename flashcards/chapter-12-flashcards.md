## Chapter 12: Full Exam Simulation and Time-Management Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Databricks Data Engineer Associate exam: total questions and time limit? | 45 questions in 90 minutes. Exam tip: budget ~2 minutes per question, leaving buffer time for flagged-question review at the end. |
| 2 | What is the passing score for the Databricks Data Engineer Associate exam? | 70%. Exam tip: use domain-level practice exam breakdowns to identify which domain is dragging your score below this threshold. |
| 3 | At the halfway point (45 min) of a 90-min, 45-question exam, how many questions should you have completed to be on pace? | ~22-23 questions. Exam tip: if you're behind this pace, stop deliberating on hard questions — flag and move on immediately. |
| 4 | What should you do with a question you're unsure about after ~2 minutes? | Select your best guess, flag it for review, and move on. Never leave a question blank — there's no benefit to skipping without an answer. |
| 5 | Elimination technique: how do you narrow down 4 options when 2 seem 'technically correct'? | Re-read the question for qualifiers like 'BEST', 'MOST cost-effective', or 'with LEAST operational overhead' — these words point to the single optimal answer among multiple valid ones. |
| 6 | 'BEST' vs 'correct' in exam wording — what's the difference? | 'Correct' options may all technically work; 'BEST' asks you to pick the most efficient, automated, or exam-preferred solution among several valid ones. Exam tip: never assume all valid-sounding answers are equally acceptable when 'BEST' is used. |
| 7 | Scenario keyword: 'automatically detect new files without reprocessing' maps to which service? | Auto Loader (cloudFiles). Exam tip: this exact phrasing is a strong signal — don't confuse with COPY INTO, which lacks built-in file-tracking state for incremental discovery. |
| 8 | Scenario keyword: 'once-daily scheduled batch load, no near-real-time need' maps to which ingestion method? | COPY INTO on a scheduled Job. Exam tip: streaming/Auto Loader is over-engineering for simple scheduled batch requirements — the exam tests recognizing when NOT to use streaming. |
| 9 | Cluster type for lowest-cost scheduled nightly ETL with no interactive use? | Job cluster (auto-created and auto-terminated by the Job). Exam tip: all-purpose clusters left running 24/7 are the classic wrong-answer trap for cost-optimization scenarios. |
| 10 | VACUUM default retention threshold? | 168 hours (7 days). Exam tip: retention below this requires disabling spark.databricks.delta.retentionDurationCheck.enabled and risks breaking time travel / concurrent reads — classic wrong-answer trap. |
| 11 | Flag-and-review strategy: when should you review flagged questions? | Only after answering all 45 questions, using remaining time. Exam tip: never interrupt forward progress mid-exam to revisit flags — this breaks pacing and risks leaving later questions unanswered. |
| 12 | Post-practice-exam review: what should you do with a domain scoring well below your overall average? | Target that domain specifically with focused review and hands-on labs using missed-question explanations, rather than spreading study time evenly. Exam tip: domain-level weakness is the highest-leverage area to fix before the real exam. |
