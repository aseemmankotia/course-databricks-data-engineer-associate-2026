# Chapter 12 Lab: Full Exam Simulation and Time-Management Strategy

**Estimated time:** 90 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a full 45-question, 90-minute timed practice exam covering all seven domains proportionally to the course's estimated emphasis, then review every missed question with its domain and explanation.

## Key takeaway

Disciplined pacing (roughly 2 minutes per question), strategic flagging, and careful reading of scenario constraints are as important to the exam outcome as domain knowledge itself.
